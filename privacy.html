Privacy Policy

We believe responsible technology starts with clear communication about how information is collected, used, and protected.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Albero Studio (“Albero Studio”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quotation, join our network, use the client portal, or interact with our digital services.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the site, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, company, selected service, project brief, budget information, message, or other details you choose to send through a contact, support, quotation, career, or newsletter form.

Information collected automatically

We may receive technical and usage information such as your IP address, browser and device details, pages visited, referring page, approximate session timing, and interactions with links or buttons. Our current site also creates anonymous session and visitor identifiers for basic analytics and stores a language preference in browser storage.

Client and account information

If you use a client account or portal, we may process account details such as your name, email address, company, login-request information, session information, and records needed to secure and operate the account.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to questions, support requests, project enquiries, and quotation requests.
  • Manage newsletter or network subscriptions and send relevant updates.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website and client services.
  • Understand anonymous website usage and improve content, navigation, and performance.
  • Prevent misuse, investigate security events, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted providers that help us host the site, operate infrastructure, process submissions, provide analytics, or support communications. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights, or to respond to a security or safety concern.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, to provide a requested service, resolve disputes, maintain business records, and meet legal or security requirements. Retention periods can vary by the type of information and the nature of the relationship.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The current frontend uses browser storage for anonymous analytics identifiers and language preference. Hosting, security, client-account, or future third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies when needed. You can manage storage and cookies through your browser settings, although some functionality may be affected.

5. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You can unsubscribe from non-essential email communications by contacting us or using an unsubscribe option where provided. You may also clear local storage or adjust browser permissions to limit anonymous identifiers.

6. Third-party links

Our website may link to social networks, external resources, or third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before sharing information.

7. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, professionals, researchers, and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version was published.
